### Explore bursaries on other teacher training routes

It’s worth checking if there’s a [bursary](/funding-and-support/bursaries) for a fee-paying route available before you apply for a PGTA. These can be worth up to $bursaries_2026_generic_max_shortened$, depending on the subject.

If you do a PGTA, you will not be eligible for a bursary or student finance. You’ll also be taxed on your income, whereas you will not be taxed on a bursary.

If you’re eligible for a bursary, you could receive more money on a fee-paying course than on a salaried training route like the PGTA.
