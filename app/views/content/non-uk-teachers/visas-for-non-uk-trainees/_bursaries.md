### Bursaries if you are on a Student visa

If you are eligible for a bursary, you can use this in your Student visa application as proof that you have enough money to support yourself.

The first instalment of a bursary is usually made before late October but you should confirm payment dates with your chosen provider.

It’s worth checking if there’s a [bursary](/funding-and-support/scholarships-and-bursaries) for a fee-paying route available before you apply for a PGTA.
